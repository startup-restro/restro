# 🌟 Customer & Loyalty

> **Module**: Customers | **Total Features**: 25 | **Phase 1 Count**: 3
> **Core Principle**: Phone-number based. No app download. Auto-profile on first order.

---

## Features

### P0 - Must Have (Phase 1)

**F-176: Phone-Based Profiles** — Create customer by phone number. No app download needed. Auto-created on first order.
**F-177: Visit History** — All past orders, total visits, total spent, average order value.
**F-193: Do Not Disturb** — Customers opt out of marketing. Respected across all channels.

### P1 - Should Have (Phase 2)

**F-178: Preference Memory** — "Extra spicy", "no cilantro", "allergic to peanuts". Shown at order time.
**F-179: Loyalty Points Earn** — Configurable: 1 point per Rs 10 spent
**F-180: Loyalty Points Redeem** — 100 points = Rs 50 off (configurable)
**F-181: Loyalty Tiers** — Bronze (0-999) → Silver (1K-2.5K) → Gold (2.5K-5K) → Platinum (5K+). Auto-upgrade with benefits.
**F-186: Feedback Collection** — Post-meal WhatsApp survey (1-5 stars + comment)
**F-187: Customer Segmentation** — Auto-segment: Regulars (weekly+), High-Spenders (top 10%), At-Risk (declining), New
**F-188: WhatsApp Marketing** — Broadcast offers to segments using approved templates. Opt-out respected.
**F-190: Customer Notes** — "VIP - window table", "wife is vegetarian"
**F-191: Customer Analytics** — New vs returning, frequency distribution, spending trends
**F-194: Customer Export** — CSV export

### P2 - Nice to Have (Phase 3+)

**F-182: Birthday Auto-Offer** | **F-183: Win-Back Campaign** | **F-184: Referral Rewards** | **F-185: Google Review Nudge** | **F-189: Gift Cards** | **F-192: Acquisition Source** | **F-195: Merge Duplicates** | **F-196: Favorite Items** | **F-197: Spending Drop Alert** | **F-198: Corporate Accounts** | **F-199: Customer Groups** | **F-200: Lifetime Value**
